FROM NEW ZEALAND, WITH LOVE

A little idea with a big heart.

Find The Cash NZ is a personal passion project built around making someone's day a little brighter. Real-world treasure hunts, everyday generosity and honest moments—shared with a community that cares.

It started with Kiwi kindness and welcomes supporters from New Zealand to the USA and beyond. Thanks for being part of it. 💚

LOOK AFTER EACH OTHER

Good fun. Good judgement.

Cash hunts should be safe, friendly and respectful.

🛑 No trespassing

Never enter private property or unsafe locations without permission.

🚗 Stay road safe

Don't stop unsafely, rush into traffic or use your phone while driving.

👨‍👩‍👧 Children with adults

Young hunters should have appropriate adult supervision.

🧡 Be kind

Respect others and only share someone's photo with their consent.
