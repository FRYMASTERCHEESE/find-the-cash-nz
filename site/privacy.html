Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

What this site collects

This companion site does not require registration and does not collect passwords, precise locations, payment details or banking information. Your optional hunt checklist is stored only in your browser's local storage. We do not receive this checklist.

Facebook and external services

Links to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube open those platforms, which apply their own privacy policies. If we later connect the official Meta Graph API to publish Page-approved updates, the website will show only public posts and links. No Facebook account passwords will be stored here.

Technical data

The GitHub Pages hosting provider may process standard network requests and security logs. Google Fonts may process requests for font files. The website may cache public page files offline using a service worker.

Children and photos

We do not collect children's details, and will not publish identifiable winner photos on this site without appropriate permission.
