Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

What this site collects

This website and Android app do not require an account or request precise location, contacts, camera, payment or banking information. The website hunt checklist is stored only in your browser. In the Android app, hunt checklist selections and optional kindness progress are saved only on your device using private preferences; we do not receive this information.

Facebook and external services

Links to Facebook and Instagram open those platforms, which apply their own privacy policies. If we later connect the official Meta Graph API to publish Page-approved updates, the website will show only public posts and links. No Facebook account passwords will be stored here.

Technical data

The Android app uses the internet only to fetch public Find The Cash NZ Page updates from this website and to open official external links. This version has no advertising or analytics SDK. GitHub Pages may process normal network requests and security logs, and external platforms may process usage under their own privacy policies. Google Fonts may process requests for website fonts. The website may cache public files for offline use.

Children and photos

We do not collect children's details, and will not publish identifiable winner photos on this site without appropriate permission.

How long information is kept and how to delete it

We do not store accounts, checklist records or kindness progress on our servers. You can clear website checklist data by resetting your checklist or clearing browser storage. You can remove Android checklist and kindness preferences by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Hosting and external platform logs are retained and deleted according to the providers' own policies.

Developer and security

Find The Cash NZ operates this website and companion app. The app does not transmit private checklist or kindness records; supported connections use HTTPS. If you have questions about how information is handled, please use the contact email below.
