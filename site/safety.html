Safe hunting guide

Always use the official Find The Cash NZ Facebook Page for current rules and announcements.

Protect people and places

  • Never trespass or search in private or restricted areas without permission.
  • Don't enter roads, waterways, construction sites, steep terrain or other hazardous places.
  • Never drive while checking clues or stop a vehicle unsafely.
  • Children should have appropriate adult supervision.
  • Respect shop staff, property owners, other hunters and wildlife.
  • Only share photos or videos of other people with their consent.

Verify before travelling

Check the actual Page post for the prize, dates, rules and whether the hunt is still active. This website does not promise that cash or prizes are available at any particular place or time. Do not send anyone money or financial information to claim a Find The Cash NZ prize.

Need help?

If you feel unsafe, leave the area. For an emergency in New Zealand, call 111. Questions: findthecashnz@gmail.com.
