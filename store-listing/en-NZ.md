**Full description**
Discover a little adventure and a whole lot of heart with Find The Cash NZ. 💚🇳🇿

Find The Cash NZ is a community project sharing real-world treasure hunts, kindness, surprises and positive stories across Aotearoa.

WHAT YOU CAN DO IN THE APP

• Open the official Facebook Page for genuine announcements, rules and hunt updates.
• Watch official Facebook reels and follow community moments.
• Prepare with a practical hunt safety checklist that is stored privately on your phone.
• Try a different offline kindness idea whenever you like.
• Record privately when you do a kind thing today.
• Read important safe-hunting advice and contact the creator with questions or partnership enquiries.

Made with Kiwi kindness. Whether you follow from New Zealand or overseas, thanks for being part of our community.

IMPORTANT: The app does not sell entries, accept payments or guarantee cash prizes. It does not detect hidden money or provide live GPS locations. Always check the original Facebook post for confirmed hunt details, eligibility and conditions. Children should participate with suitable adult supervision.
